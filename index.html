Douglas County School District

The Douglas County School District (DCSD) is Colorado's third-largest school district, serving over 60,000 students in preschool through 12th grade. It is known for its wide range of educational choices, including open enrollment, charter schools, and a variety of programs like International Baccalaureate and Advanced Placement courses. DCSD also offers numerous Career and Technical Education (CTE) and concurrent enrollment pathways, preparing students for college, career, or military service.

Parker Government Website

Parker, located in Douglas County with easy access to the Denver metropolitan area, is known for its unique Western-Victorian downtown and its hometown feel. Their website has information on Parker's elections, political processes, social events, and community programs. Residents looking for information on homeowning and public safety can easily find dedicated resources. Additionally, their website features the Town Council agendas, budget transparency tools, and project updates, giving the community insight into future development.

The Parker Recreation Center

The Parker Recreation Center, located at 17301 Lincoln Ave, Parker, CO 80134, offers a wide range of fitness, wellness, and recreational opportunities for residents of all ages. The facility includes a gymnasium, fitness studios, an indoor pool, and various sports courts. They provide numerous sports programs, classes, and events that promote a healthy lifestyle for youths and adults. Their website provides information of membership, class schedules, rentals, and special events. The Parker Recreation Center plays a key role in promoting an active lifestyle throughout the whole Town of Parker
